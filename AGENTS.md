- Public storytelling routes use continuous WebGL scroll narratives with route-specific camera paths and scenes, because the reference experience is cinematic rather than stacked sections.
- The homepage composes its filament and photographic worlds with HomeNarrative and a dedicated spatial HomeProofWorld; this separates cinematic geometry from editorial composition while allowing a radical presentation redesign.
- The homepage opening uses a scoped HomeOpening editorial sequence driven by the existing chapter progress; this shares the auditor typography language without changing later cinematic worlds or their timeline.
- Homepage chapter boundaries, scene visibility and narrative composition share the pure home timeline so forward scrolling, reverse scrolling and navigation use identical scene windows.

- DNA and transition networks live in LuminousScenes with analytic round sprites; shared target-position shader deformation stretches departing fibres into straight constellation edges. The same strand morphs from a supplier globe at the opening, returns in the expert chapter and morphs back into the supplier globe in the pilot chapter, driven by the home timeline rather than swapping images.
- The Atlas showcase uses CDN-hosted full-length recordings with preserved audio and scroll-selected windows; contain-fit playback preserves the complete UI and sound controls handle browser autoplay restrictions.
- The expert chapter is a pure DNA-particle constellation with no geographic map, because the user rejected map overlays as cheap.
- How it works renders image-sampled pixels in a scoped WebGL canvas; pixel assembly and photograph reveal share the orbit's interpolated level position so scrolling and direct level selection stay synchronized without a DNA strand.
- The auditor route uses one client-only WebGL fibre world driven by a pure reversible journey score shared with concise DOM chapters; generated digital face samples morph into homepage-matched DNA and later network geometry, while source details stay in dialogs and homepage rendering remains untouched.
